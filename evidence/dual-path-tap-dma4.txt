== 8. The recorder fixed (later the same day)
Two causes, each confirmed:
- File writes on the USB thread: path A (no --path ab), 3 s to disk, missed the deadline in 4 of 4
  runs (207-545 us late). wsdr_probe stream now copies each bank into a 2048-bank ring and a writer
  thread unpacks and writes; the writer stayed within 2 banks (queued <= 2) in every run.
- The URB depth: the probe posted 32 (~1.3 ms at 40 MS/s; the Receiver posts 256). With the writer
  thread and depth 32, --path ab 30 s failed 2 of 2 (framing: part 3 len 168 desc 90.00.00.80... at
  bank 38983; deadline 1102.8 us late at bank 67673); with depth 256, clean. The probe now uses 256
  at >= 20 MS/s.
Both fixed, --path ab --seconds 30 three times and --seconds 60 once: ok 1, 146484 / 292969 banks,
0 strays, late_max 0.8 us, end_max 182.8 us; path A 3 s 5 of 5. (The 168-byte packets were not seen
with depth 256: a side effect of the starved queue, not a source to filter.)
